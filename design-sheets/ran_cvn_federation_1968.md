# Federation class — 1960s late

**Sea Power design edition:** 1960s late (1968); nuclear propulsion; 68 aircraft.

The hull is original geometry using US Enterprise nuclear carrier as a design cue. The proposed ship is alternate history. Length 342 m, flight-deck width 78 m, estimated displacement 89,000 t, design speed 32 kn. The deck plan has 4 physical lifts and 4 indicated catapult tracks. These positions still need native taxi, launch and recovery routing.
