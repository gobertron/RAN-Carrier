# Australia class — 1960s early

**Sea Power design edition:** 1960s early (1963); steam propulsion; 68 aircraft.

The hull is original geometry using US Kitty Hawk conventional CATOBAR as a design cue. The proposed ship is alternate history. Length 326 m, flight-deck width 86 m, estimated displacement 82,000 t, design speed 32 kn. The deck plan has 4 physical lifts and 4 indicated catapult tracks. These positions still need native taxi, launch and recovery routing.
