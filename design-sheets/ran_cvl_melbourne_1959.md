**Sea Power design edition:** 1950s late (1959); steam propulsion; 30 aircraft.

The proposed ship is alternate history. Length 217 m, flight-deck width 38 m, estimated displacement 20,000 t, design speed 25 kn. The deck plan has 2 physical lifts and 2 indicated catapult tracks. These positions still need native taxi, launch and recovery routing.
