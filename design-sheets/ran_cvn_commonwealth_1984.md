# Commonwealth class — 1980s early

**Sea Power design edition:** 1980s early (1984); nuclear propulsion; 98 aircraft.

The hull is original geometry using US Nimitz design with enlarged Australian deck and island as a design cue. The proposed ship is alternate history. Length 350 m, flight-deck width 88 m, estimated displacement 104,000 t, design speed 32 kn. The deck plan has 4 physical lifts and 4 indicated catapult tracks. These positions still need native taxi, launch and recovery routing.
