# Credits and asset provenance

The fourteen RAN hull OBJ and MTL files, scaled derivatives, design data and rendered previews are original work created for this package. British and US carrier families are historical **design cues**; no donor model, texture or definition is repackaged.

For approximate OBJ scale calibration only, the earlier supplied `ran_majestic_melb98.ini` (217 m length) and `Melbourne_test_3.obj` (3.161967 Z units) were measured. These reference files came from [Project Southern Cross, Workshop item 3574957049](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3574957049), whose page lists **Peter Garrett** as creator. The source mod itself credits additional asset contributors; the specific Majestic model's full asset lineage remains to be checked before any third-party asset reuse. No third-party bytes are included here.

Additional installed Workshop carrier assets should retain their own maker names and permissions. No installed Workshop asset was copied into this design archive.
