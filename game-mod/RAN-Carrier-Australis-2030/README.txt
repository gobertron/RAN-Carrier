RAN Australis 2030 Editor Prototype
This is an opt-in editor test of an original mesh and native-style carrier deck routes. The air group is user-selected and starts empty. Targets: 99 aircraft, 5300 embarked, 5800 berths, 112000 t full load. Five 95 m steam CATOBAR tracks: three bow and two shallow 6-degree port-waist lanes, staggered 21 m. Flush metal tracks, small shuttles, white guides and segmented deflectors are static visual details. All five rolling lanes accept Plane, VTOL and STOL; four dedicated spots accept Helicopter and VTOL. Automatic discovery accepts correctly installed carrier-capable aircraft with no class, size, role or loadout exclusions. Combat-fit.json declares modern targets and stock proxies: Mk41 with Sea Sparrow defensive rounds, plus Sea Sparrow in the ESSM and 21-round RAM-form launchers, Phalanx, 30 mm, SPY-1A, SLQ-32, original offensive ECM, SRBOC and two Nixie decoys. Nulka and lasers remain visual positions. New combat behaviour and flight-deck animation require in-game testing. A stock Sea Power Nimitz bundle is named as a runtime fallback; no stock or Workshop asset bytes are copied.
The user confirmed mission loading and original hull rendering on 22 October 2026 after the Foam section was added. This revision uses an 82 m main deck and four offset helicopter spots ahead of the islands. The port lift sits beside the aft part of the main island at z=-32 m, behind the waist launch run. Four 22 x 26 m lifts use centred ride/spawn positions and fore-and-aft parking, checked against the stock F-14A reference. All five catapult staging positions and aligned approaches are checked with A-7E, F-14A and a 25 m span x 30 m length reference envelope. LaunchDelay=1 is a shared one-second minimum, not a guarantee of synchronized launches. Bow 1/3 can be paired; waist launches block all bow lanes, each other and all four vertical spots. All four vertical spots block waist lanes 4/5, leaving bow lanes available. All recovery points explicitly block all launches. Native eligibility does not verify every aircraft model: swept taxi turns, rotor clearance and mixed deck operations still need game QA.
The rear island is narrower, shorter and lower with six-face wraparound glazing. The arrested-recovery axis is 9 degrees to port and its wires are perpendicular. Four defensive VLS banks and four missile housings occupy main-deck edge positions with NoPark zones. RIM-66E and RGM-109 are removed. Sea Sparrow retains stock guidance; the vertical-launch proxy requires in-game checking. Three CIWS mounts now hide in covered deck-edge wells, open in 0.4 seconds, and fire after the native opening animation completes. Retraction takes 1 second after the native idle/rest condition (over 10 seconds). In-game QA is pending. All five guns use original moving yaw bodies and elevation barrels. The normal 30 mm guns now provide NumberOfGuns/Gun1, correcting the null barrel that caused WeaponSystemGun.AlignTurret to fail. This correction still needs an in-game firing test.
Aircraft contact points use the actual deck/pad/lift surface heights; the former 16 cm taxi/launch offset is removed. Aviation stores now contain 6000000/6000000 ammo points, with no individual ammunition-category caps. Stock aircraft gun and countermeasure capacities are retained. Select an armed loadout and Ready the aircraft. Use a fresh carrier in a new mission so saved ammunition and old deck positions do not override this revision.
The rebuilt deck has lift platforms and painted helipads at 22.002 m, only 2 mm above the main deck. Contact coordinates follow those exported surfaces. Ordinary deck markings are millimetres thick; stowed blast deflectors and closed laser hatches sit within 2 mm of the deck. Visible arresting wires rise 3 cm and launch shuttles 6 cm above deck.
