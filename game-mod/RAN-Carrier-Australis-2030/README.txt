RAN Australis 2030 Editor Prototype
This is an opt-in editor test of an original mesh and native-style carrier deck routes. The air group is user-selected and starts empty. Targets: 99 aircraft, 5300 embarked, 5800 berths, 112000 t full load. Five 95 m steam CATOBAR tracks: three bow and two angled port-waist. The waist pair currently accepts the stock A-7E; three bow lanes accept other carrier aircraft. Combat-fit.json declares modern targets and stock proxies: Mk41/SM-2/Tomahawk, Sea Sparrow in the ESSM and 21-round RAM-form launchers, Phalanx, 30 mm, SPY-1A, SLQ-32, original offensive ECM, SRBOC and two Nixie decoys. Nulka and lasers remain visual positions. New combat behaviour and flight-deck animation require in-game testing. A stock Sea Power Nimitz bundle is named as a runtime fallback; no stock or Workshop asset bytes are copied.
The user confirmed mission loading and original hull rendering on 22 October 2026 after the Foam section was added. This revision uses an 82 m main deck and four independent aft helicopter spots. Four 22 x 26 m lifts use centred ride/spawn positions and fore-and-aft parking, checked against the stock F-14A reference. Catapult staging is checked using the stock A-7E. LaunchDelay=1 is a shared one-second minimum, not a guarantee of synchronized launches. Bow 1/3 can be paired; waist launches block all bow lanes and each other. Recovery blocks all launches. Flight operations need game QA.
