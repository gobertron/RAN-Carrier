# Australis v0.5.9 — defensive platforms and helicopter operations

> Archived v0.5.9 notes. See the [current project guide](../../README.md) for v0.8.2 and current downloads.

Deck defensive platforms stop 0.08 m above the 22 m deck, replacing the former 24 m base height. Deployment still takes 0.75 simulation seconds after committed aircraft clear; the 300-second last-shot hold and 10-second stow remain.

The Phalanx count rises from three to five. The two additional mounts retract inside the main and rear tower roofs. Their split covers fold inward before the gun platforms rise. Tower caps are reshaped to enclose the pockets; the rear bridge retains six-face glazing. Roof CIWS bases stop 0.08 m above their roofs. The original CIWS between the towers moves to starboard (29.4,-142) m, behind the aft pad and beside the aft VLS bank. Its covers slide fore/aft to clear the adjacent VLS covers. Shared 20 mm ammunition rises to 20,000 rounds.

Helicopter launch preference is **aft pad, front pad, middle pad** (launch points 6,4,5). The controller orders only each helicopter/VTOL pending task's elevator list, preserving the actual elevator objects, native reservations and the ship's global list. Lift 4's associations are ordered aft then middle, and both helicopter lifts require a free launch point. Recovery points enumerate aft, front, middle after the fixed-wing point. Different pads no longer block each other; launch and recovery at the *same* pad still conflict. Two helicopter lifts feed three independent pads, so shared-lift movements can queue even while the pads operate concurrently.

Native Nimitz-reference deck-taxi velocities are configured: forward 15, backward -10, slow 10. LaunchDelay=1 and SpawnInterval=1 use simulation seconds. Native takeoff physics, aircraft warm-up/rotor spin-up, sea-state checks and heading checks remain. Code inspection found no Full/Flank carrier-power prerequisite in the fixed-wing/rotary takeoff gates; fixed-wing launches require the ship within two degrees of its ordered course. **Standard-power and 1x-speed behaviour still need an in-game check.** This release does not force the carrier to full power or alter other aircraft.

The four-lift/three-pad layout, aircraft on lifts 1/2 and helicopter/VTOL on lifts 3/4, five repair teams and previous motion tuning are retained. Only the independent carrier and its controller are installed. Use a fresh carrier in a blank mission after installing; check platform heights, roof covers, firing, pad preference, simultaneous pad activity, normal-time operations and rollback backups. No live Sea Power test is claimed.
